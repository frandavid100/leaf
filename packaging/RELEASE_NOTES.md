Leaf 0.1.0-alpha.25 refines comments, style previews, page thumbnails and rulers.

- Comment threads collapse outside their anchor. Their headers show a compact relative age and the full date on hover.
- Style names preview their resolved typeface and emphasis at the interface text size.
- Page thumbnails stay packed at the top of the sidebar.
- The lighter rulers keep their hover state on the correct sheet and accept scrolling. Drag a white/grey boundary to adjust the section margin.
- The language menu indicates Hunspell files detected by WordCraft. A verified system language-settings integration remains pending.

Install Leaf-0.1.0-alpha.25-x86_64.flatpak over alpha.24. Leaf is experimental and its development has used AI assistance.
