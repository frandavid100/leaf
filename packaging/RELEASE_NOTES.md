Leaf 0.1.0-alpha.30 includes the complete alpha.29 candidate and a further issue pass.

- Active page and thumbnail follow the document caret, including repagination. Scrolling the canvas or browsing thumbnails no longer changes the editing target.
- Picture edits preserve their viewport through page/section rebuilds. Showing a selected image follows its actual bounds rather than an unrelated text caret; deferred refreshes reject stale document revisions.
- Image and ruler cursors reflect selection, move and resize targets, remain stable during drags, and reset on leaving or cancelling. Image handles take priority over table/body cursors.
- Ordinary clicks on automatic contents entries follow their stored heading bookmarks. Hover shows a pointer; a temporary theme-aware editor cue identifies the index without affecting saved documents or PDF output.
- Corrections, suggested changes and comment threads use the same flat, theme-aware conversation card background.
- Text uses symbolic icons, continuous native linked property/reapply rows and a full-width Size control. Font has an internal page retaining direct family entry and previews. Font and Language remember their own last successful settings. Superscript moves to More Text Options.
- Paragraph uses the same property/reapply rows. Increase/Decrease Indent move inside Indents; Borders and Shading share a page and repeat their combined configuration. More Options stays icon-free. Reapplying a multilevel list preserves both its type and its level in one undoable edit.
- Elements keeps existing-object properties visible but insensitive without a target, explains availability with tooltips, and puts insertion last. Header and context entry points still share the same controls.
- Navigation now has one primary entry with internal Pages and Structure representations. Each document retains its filter, collapsed headings and independent sidebar scroll positions; returning from Review or Search restores the last representation. A destination changed while Structure is hidden is revealed on return.
- Navigation adds a read-only type filter and canonical entries for images, tables, contents, captions, notes, equations, links and fields, including nested table cells. Filters retain the relevant heading/section context; active entries use real link/object/note extents. Caption/object ownership remains unfinished, so #124 stays open.

Also included from the unpublished alpha.29 candidate: unified Review with ordered corrections, tracked changes and comment threads; Edit/Suggest mode; mixed-state formatting; inheritable proofing languages; section-aware footnotes; automatic references and cross-references; 0.5 cm ruler snapping; translucent image selection and eight resize handles; improved thumbnail reveal; and the basic UnicodeMath equation editor.

Known limits: #121 remains open for the caption-property dependency, and #124 for the remaining navigation details. #126/#127 need a durable canonical image/table-caption association before a reversible Show Caption property and connected decoration can be reliable; the existing direct caption insertion remains available. The pinned engine supports one automatic table of contents per document. Explicit insertion never silently replaces an existing index. The original footnote placement/missing-number cases still need their source document to reproduce. Floating-image placement on a paragraph beginning with a manual page break remains an engine defect; Leaf rejects that placement and restores the original. Unequal columns and selected-text continuous sections retain their documented engine limitations. Advanced references, notes, floating images and equations require DOCX to preserve their structure.

David authorized automatic publication for this pass. Publication promotes the exact validated candidate files without rebuilding; candidate.json, source-commit.txt and SHA256SUMS identify them. The regular bundle uses GNOME 49; the FlatPark archive is checked under GNOME 51. Automated validation does not imply human testing. Leaf is experimental and its development has used substantial AI assistance.
