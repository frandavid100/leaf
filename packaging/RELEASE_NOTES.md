Leaf 0.1.0-alpha.26 refines sections, comments and rulers.

- Each section starts a new page row. Rows use the section’s page dimensions and the current zoom.
- Drag any of the four section margins on the rulers. The ruler band moves during the drag, and Undo restores the previous margin in one step.
- Scroll the document over visible rulers. Ruler labels, tick marks and the band separator share the same grey.
- Comment cards use a lighter background, regular body text, and clearer reply separators and connectors.
- The section menu uses the same spacing as the spelling menu.

Install Leaf-0.1.0-alpha.26-x86_64.flatpak over alpha.25. Leaf is experimental and its development has used AI assistance.
