# Nautilus search options symbol

`hicolor/scalable/actions/nautilus-search-filters-symbolic.svg` is the unmodified
GNOME Nautilus search-filters symbol, from the GNOME project:
https://github.com/GNOME/nautilus/blob/main/src/resources/icons/scalable/actions/nautilus-search-filters-symbolic.svg

Distributed under Nautilus's GNU GPL version 3 or later, compatible with Leaf's
GPL-3.0-or-later distribution. Nautilus copyright and licensing:
https://github.com/GNOME/nautilus/blob/main/LICENSE
The complete GPLv3 license accompanies Leaf in `LICENSE`.

Leaf bundles this symbol in its own icon search path. The Flatpak does not rely
on Nautilus being installed or on access to Nautilus's private icon resources.
